# Family Force: Shadow Grid — Story Bible

## Canon

- Arabic title: **قوة العائلة: شبكة الظل**
- English title: **Family Force: Shadow Grid**
- Company: **NexHome Robotics**
- Antagonist: **Adrian Vox**
- Malware: **Shadow Code / شفرة الظل**
- Moral center: technology is a responsibility; protecting people never means
  controlling them.

NexHome sells affordable domestic robots for homes, shops, transit and public
safety. Vox secretly ships every unit with a dormant backdoor. Once enough
robots are deployed, Shadow Code activates and connects them to one command
network. Essa, a safety and cybersecurity engineer, detects the hidden traffic,
reports it lawfully and develops a cleanup patch. Vox activates the network
early and targets Essa's family before the patch can be broadcast.

## Hero voices

- **Essa:** calm, ethical and protective. He explains before he strikes and
  always attempts to free a robot rather than destroy it.
- **Adam:** brave, joyful and impulsive. His arc is learning that strength is
  most useful when guided by thought.
- **Shaikha:** observant, precise and composed. She identifies signals, traps
  and weaknesses others miss.
- **Sulaiman:** confident, quick and encouraging. He scouts routes and restores
  the team's hope under pressure.

## Villain voices

- **Adrian Vox:** polite, controlled and convinced that freedom is inefficient.
- **Keeper-7:** security language; treats citizens as unauthorized risks.
- **Railmaster-9:** schedules, delays and forbidden passengers.
- **Tidebreaker:** cargo and shipping language; values the shipment over lives.
- **Control Titan:** Vox's final machine; repeats the command `ONE NETWORK`.

## Campaign structure

### Stage 1 — Neon Market

Shadow Code activates in public. The family protects civilians and traces the
first command relay. Keeper-7 is cleaned after defeat and reveals that the
commands came through Transit Terminal.

### Stage 2 — Transit Terminal

The robots move server hardware aboard an armored train. The family stops
Railmaster-9 and learns that thousands of compromised units are stored at Moon
Harbor for export.

### Stage 3 — Moon Harbor

The team reaches a broadcast tower and uploads Essa's cleanup patch. Vox jams
the signal through Tidebreaker. The partial broadcast frees many domestic
robots, forcing Vox to retreat to the original factory.

### Stage 4 — Junk Palace

Vox combines discarded machines into Control Titan and attempts to seize the
city permanently. The heroes use Family Link to open a broadcast window, Essa
finishes the patch and the robot network is freed rather than destroyed.

## Ending

The restored robots help repair the city. Vox is taken into custody and the
evidence is preserved. Essa tells the children: `Strength and knowledge are a
responsibility. We use them to help people, never to control them.`

## Presentation rules

- All story screens are skippable; no video is required.
- Arabic is true RTL and English is LTR.
- No gameplay damage, scoring or timers advance while dialogue is open.
- All four heroes remain present in canon; the selected P1/P2 pair takes the
  frontline positions.
- Boss defeat language says `freed`, `cleaned` or `disconnected`, not killed.
- Every scene has the same stable ID in both languages.
