# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no server or external runtime. Inferred from the explicit request for a local multi-tab HTML guide.

## Users

The primary user is the Family Force producer or production assistant preparing personalized birthday-event APK orders. Customers are a secondary audience when the order requirements are printed or shared.

## Product Purpose

Provide one operational source of truth for intake, quoting, asset production, quality control, APK build, privacy, and delivery. Success means a new order can be specified and produced without guessing asset counts or editing the Android engine.

## Positioning

The guide maps a personalized family game order directly to the engine's exact character, animation, weapon, stage, branding, consent, and APK contracts.

## Operating Context

Used locally alongside `customers/`, the Android asset factory, validators, signed Release builder, and customer delivery reports. It must work offline and print cleanly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic RTL multi-tab guide.
- Current engine requires four hero slots and fixed hero animation atlases.
- Image-only character production; video generation is prohibited.
- APK delivery is direct, not through an app store.
- Customer photos, especially minors' photos, require recorded consent and must not be packaged raw.
- Prices are business decisions and must not be fabricated by the guide.

## Brand Commitments

Family Force: Neon Streets; joyful high-resolution retro brawler language; cyan, pink, gold, and midnight-blue identity; direct, practical Arabic copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the Android game, current customer pack, schemas, image-only character factory, APK builder, privacy runbooks, and final QA reports. No approved public price list exists.

## Product Principles

- Exact production contracts beat general advice.
- The customer sees simple requests; internal technical complexity stays in production.
- Consent and privacy gates cannot be bypassed or pre-filled dishonestly.
- Every paid addition has an explicit input, output, validation gate, and effort band.
- The guide remains usable offline and printable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible tabs, visible focus, strong contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, semantic headings, and print output are required.
